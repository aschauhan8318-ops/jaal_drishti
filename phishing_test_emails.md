# Phishing Test Emails

80 fictional phishing emails for testing JaalDrishti: 10 for each of the 8 attack types
the app recognises. Every email here is phishing, so the expected result is
**Phishing** or at least **Suspicious**.

Difficulty:
- **Easy** – obvious scam (spelling, prizes, shouting)
- **Medium** – looks like a real company notice, but has a bad link or request
- **Hard** – polite and realistic, often no link; relies on context

All names, domains, phone numbers and account numbers are made up.
